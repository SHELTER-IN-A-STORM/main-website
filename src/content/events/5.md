# 3rd Annual Fall Fundraiser

**Date:** Saturday, November 14, 2026
**Time:** 5:00 PM - 8:00 PM
**Location:** Memory Lane Events, 78 Walnut Grove Road, Eldon, MO

Join us for an unforgettable evening supporting Shelter in a Storm! Enjoy great food, friendly competition, and fun for a wonderful cause.

- **Dip-Off Competition** — Register to compete with your favorite dip recipe
